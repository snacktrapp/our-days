All three CI build jobs use those scripts without uploading artifacts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Environment validation prevents known credentials from entering the web process, while artifact scanning detects accidental source/build inclusion and private fixture serialization. | ADR-027 | Invitation links carry the raw one-time secret in the URL fragment. The database persists only the token hash.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | URL fragments are not sent in ordinary HTTP requests or referrers; the short-lived server-only intent survives an iPhone Mail/PWA reload without leaving the secret in React state, form fields, storage, or a normal navigation URL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ADR-028 | Written moments keep `circle_id`, journal person, and recorder immutable. All ordinary writes use narrow RPCs with optimistic revisions; browsers cannot hard-delete.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | A family member's journal ownership and the person who recorded a child's memory remain truthful under edits, races, and direct database calls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Mobile pagination may cumulatively re-read bounded pages for the initial interaction, but must become incremental before scale makes that costly.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Backdated inserts and concurrent edits cannot silently enter an in-progress journey, and equal timestamps do not duplicate or omit memories.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

| ADR-031 | MVP places are explicit, trimmed labels stored on the protected moment. The app does not request GPS, infer EXIF location, call a geocoder/map service, or normalize labels into a shared place directory.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | A useful “where” should not introduce invisible tracking, third-party disclosure, or false map precision; a private place index can be added later from the same labels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

The three stable semantic IDs may receive revised display copy before production.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | One quiet response matches the approved interaction and avoids accumulating engagement-style metrics while keeping display wording reversible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ADR-033 | Notes and reaction identities are fetched through a separately authorized, no-store detail action only after a moment is opened; timeline rows never serialize closed conversations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Family notes are more private than timeline summaries and should not be carried through initial RSC payloads, browser history, or pages the member never opened.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ADR-034 | Connected Family Settings may show active account/managed-profile access, minimal pending-invitation metadata, and organizer-only revoke controls. It does not create invitations or receive an email address/raw token until a separate trusted worker can provision the Auth account and deliver the one-time secret. Every mutation is scoped to the server-derived active circle before the database RPC independently rechecks authority.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A raw invite that cannot be delivered safely is a stranded bearer secret. Server-derived circle scoping also prevents a future dual-circle organizer from changing the wrong family while using one circle's UI.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ADR-035 | Milestones use a dedicated, authenticated, circle-authorized archive RPC and the established snapshot-plus-keyset journey contract. The Memories landing offers one count-free Milestones doorway beneath On This Day; the route reuses the central timeline and existing moment actions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | A dedicated static query preserves the mature year/anniversary contract, admits a milestone-only partial index, and avoids client filtering or engagement-style archive counts. Reuse keeps milestones visibly part of family history instead of making them a separate feature feed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ADR-036 | Organizer role changes and explicit child-journal guardian assignments use narrow Server Actions plus independently authorized database RPCs. Organizers implicitly care for every managed child journal; explicit guardian assignments survive demotion. Desired-state retries and already-completed membership removals reconcile without duplicate audit history, and role audits record promotion versus demotion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | This implements PD-001/PD-002 without granting organizers edit authority over another adult's journal. Circle-first locks, server-derived target scoping, idempotent recovery, and directional audits make access changes safe under stale screens, lost responses, and concurrent requests.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ADR-037 | Whole-family exports begin as a private, circle-bound organizer request ledger with one queued request per requester and an immutable authorization version. Demotion or revocation terminally invalidates queued work. It prevents stale authority, request flooding, caller-fabricated jobs, and partial-work publication from becoming production defaults.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ADR-038 | Invitation delivery begins with a private, circle-bound request ledger keyed to a provisioned target Auth account and the requester's exact organizer authorization generation. The browser receives only an opaque job ID; the ledger stores no address or bearer secret. A separately deployed worker will derive the one-time token from a versioned worker-only key, bind the eventual invitation to the exact target account, and use a durable provider-idempotency boundary. Delivery remains disabled until real coordinator, provider, revocation, and receipt schemas pass integration tests.                                                                                                                               | This implements the accepted PD-003 direction without putting email addresses, raw tokens, Auth Admin credentials, or partially delivered invitations in the web application. It also makes demotion, removal, target activation, lost responses, and retries explicit state transitions instead of best-effort request behavior.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ADR-039 | Durable moment authorship and trash attribution use same-circle membership IDs, never Auth user IDs. Public timeline, Memories, and milestone responses continue to expose the family-facing recorder person, not a membership or sign-in identifier. Auth-account detachment and deletion remain a later disabled workflow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Memberships and people are the lasting family-history identities; Auth users are replaceable access attachments. This cutover preserves truthful attribution and tenant integrity while removing the only per-moment dependency that would otherwise block future account closure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ADR-040 | Recovery starts with a values-free, synthetic-only logical database fidelity drill that restores into a temporary database in the same local Supabase Postgres container. Production recovery remains gated on a quarantined new-project rehearsal at the current migration head, session and credential invalidation, an external append-only suppression ledger, and a separate authenticated/encrypted media backup with full-byte SHA-256 reconciliation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Same-container restore evidence catches schema/data regressions without pretending that a database dump contains Storage object bytes or hosted configuration. Keeping the stronger production gates explicit prevents a local test from being mistaken for disaster recovery, deletion non-resurrection, or an RPO/RTO commitment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ADR-041 | Account closure begins with an immutable, private, Auth-independent request ledger and a narrow trusted preparation transaction. Requesting pauses new export/invitation work but leaves ordinary family access intact; preparation rechecks every circle, preserves family-history attribution, revokes access, terminalizes outstanding work, and detaches retained memberships from Auth. A surviving organizer must be active, Auth-attached, and not closing. Auth Admin deletion, media reconciliation, external restore suppression, and UI remain disabled.                                                                                                                                                                   | Separating durable intent from atomic authorization closure avoids deleting shared family history, stranding two simultaneous co-organizer requests, or trusting a stale web session. Keeping the irreversible external steps behind later gates prevents a database-only foundation from being mistaken for complete account deletion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ADR-042 | Photo intake begins with a third private `our-days-intake` quarantine bucket and an identity-immutable, monotonic private ledger. Before upload, the client commits a claim that binds the exact opaque path to its declared full-byte SHA-256, byte count, and MIME type. An authenticated browser may use its ordinary JWT only for direct TUS create/part operations at that live claimed path; standard and signed uploads, upsert, S3, read/list, update, and delete remain unavailable. A completed transfer becomes only `uploaded_unverified`. Membership, Auth attachment, closure, and effective journal authority are rechecked; relevant authority loss blocks later requests and terminally invalidates affected intake. | Vercel's 4.5 MiB request-body limit rules out using the web deployment as an original-quality upload proxy. Supabase Storage's pinned TUS implementation can nevertheless allow two concurrent upload URLs for one path to finish successfully, so neither the claim nor a quarantine object proves a physical single write. Quarantine is therefore never publishable. A future isolated validator must close browser upload authority, download and hash/decode the bytes, copy the exact verified bytes to a fresh immutable canonical path that browsers cannot write, and publish only that canonical object. Late quarantine writes are harmless to published bytes and must be swept. |
| ADR-043 | Invitation delivery advances through a local-only target-bound materialization seam before any account provisioner, provider adapter, worker deployment, or Send UI exists. A job may materialize at most one invitation whose immutable target is the job's exact Auth UUID; email confirmation and a salted email digest remain defense in depth, never the membership authority. New coordinator functions stay private and ungranted, including from `service_role`, until a separately deployed worker boundary is reviewed. | The Phase 2B request ledger names an existing target account, while the legacy invitation path grants by bearer plus email and exposes raw-token creation/preflight seams. Exact target binding closes same-email account substitution and makes job/invitation invalidation atomic without prematurely choosing provider infrastructure. Keeping the coordinator unreachable prevents database groundwork from being mistaken for working delivery; legacy seams remain explicit production blockers until the full email-to-auth cutover replaces them. |
| ADR-044 | Photo verification uses separately deployed, network-restricted validator identities with no family membership and no general service key. Allowlisting and later membership attachment are mutually exclusive, validator revocation serializes with every coordinator, and an expired lease must move to a different validator identity with a fresh random attempt and canonical path. A validator downloads quarantine to a bounded private spool, incrementally counts and hashes the complete bytes, structurally validates and fully decodes only the reviewed image formats, uploads those exact bytes without upsert to the attempt's browser-unwritable canonical path, downloads the canonical object again, and only then atomically records one immutable original. The local Phase 4B foundation exposes no photo moment, derivative, browser media read, signed URL, export inclusion, cleanup promise, or production worker. | A previously authorized TUS completion can still change quarantine after acknowledgement, so validating and later copying by mutable source path is unsafe. A private spool makes the verified bytes the upload source; a fresh attempt-specific no-upsert path and canonical read-back make late quarantine writes and stale validator attempts irrelevant and make crash recovery fail closed. A narrowly allowlisted validator identity keeps Storage RLS meaningful, while stock Vercel/Next image tooling does not yet prove iPhone HEIC decoding. JPEG, PNG, and WebP may advance locally; HEIC, hosted process isolation and whole-invocation deadlines, cleanup, byte recovery, and media delivery remain explicit gates. |
| ADR-045 | Display preparation reuses the isolated no-family validator boundary but leases the exact immutable original identity and version. It fully revalidates the source, applies orientation, constrains geometry and output size, converts to sRGB WebP without EXIF/GPS/XMP/ICC/IPTC, and uploads without upsert to a fresh derivative/attempt path in a private 12 MiB WebP-only bucket. The validator re-downloads, recounts, rehashes, structurally parses, and fully decodes that exact output before an atomic coordinator binds its checksum, geometry, Storage identity/version, transform profile, and immutable upload metadata into a private derivative ledger. Phase 4C grants no family read or delivery capability. | A successful image encoder call is not publication evidence: stale attempts, path collisions, truncated transfers, changed bytes, or false metadata claims must not create a usable derivative. Exact attempt paths plus canonical read-back make publication fail closed, while keeping family delivery absent avoids silently deciding PD-006 cache and revocation semantics. The same boundary intentionally leaves cleanup/purge under PD-005 and hosted process isolation, genuine iPhone/HEIC evidence, media recovery, and production activation for later gates. |

## Product decisions

Before a consuming phase begins, its decision must be `accepted` with approver and date; tests and policies cite that accepted ID.

| PD-002 | **Accepted — Brian — 2026-08-30** | Adults control permanent deletion of their own moments; organizers manage access, exports, guardians, and managed-child content but cannot silently rewrite another adult's words. | Organizer power does not erase adult authorship.                                                                                                                |
| PD-003 | **Accepted — Brian — 2026-08-30** | Start with invitation-bound emailed one-time codes/links, with ordinary OTP configured not to create users.                                                                        | Low-friction sign-in without open registration; passkeys can be reconsidered when mature.                                                                       |
