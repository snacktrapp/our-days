# Our Days Support

**Email us:** team@beelinetech.co. We usually reply within 1–2 business days. Reports of abuse are reviewed within 24 hours.

## Common questions
- **I can't sign in.** Enter the email address your invitation was sent to, then type the 6-digit code from the email. Codes expire after 1 hour. Check your spam folder for mail from ourdays@mail.beelinetech.co.
- **How do I join a family's journal?** Ask a circle organizer to invite you. Then open the invitation email, or tap "Join with an invitation" on the sign-in screen.
- **How do I report a post or comment?** Tap the ••• menu on the post or comment and choose **Report**.
- **How do I block someone?** From a post or comment by that person, open the ••• menu and choose **Block [name]**. You'll stop seeing their posts and comments. To undo it, go to Settings → Blocked people.
- **How do I delete my account?** In the app, go to **Settings → Delete account**. Your account, and the posts, photos, videos, comments, and hearts you added, are deleted within 7 days. We email you when it's done.
