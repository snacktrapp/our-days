# Our Days Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE OF PUBLICATION]  ·  **Version:** 2026-10-draft-1

Our Days is a private family journal. It is offered by [Beeline Tech — confirm exact legal entity name and state, e.g. "Beeline Tech LLC, a California limited liability company"] ("**we**", "**us**"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Our Days iPhone app and the Our Days website (together, the "**Service**"), why we collect it, who helps us run the Service, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: **team@beelinetech.co**.

## 1. The short version
- Our Days is invite-only. What you post is visible only to the people in the circles you post to (or only to you, for "Just me" posts).
- We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We use a small number of service providers (listed below) only to run the Service.
- You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). Deletion removes your account and the posts, photos, videos, comments, and hearts you added, within 7 days.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us**
- **Account information:** your email address and the name shown to your family. If you sign in on the website with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
- **Content you add:** posts ("moments"), photos, videos, captions, quotes, links (for example YouTube links), comments, hearts and reactions, drafts, people tags and mentions, and profile choices such as your color.
- **Information about other people:** when you invite someone, you give us their name and email address. When you post, you may include names, photos, and videos of family members, including children (see section 7).
- **Reports and messages:** if you report content or contact us, we keep what you send.

**Information collected when you use a feature**
- **Location (optional):** only when you choose to add a place to a post. With your permission, the app reads your device's location to suggest a place name. The place name and its coordinates are saved with that post. We never collect location in the background.
- **Photos, camera, and microphone (optional):** only for media you choose to add or record. We upload only the items you pick.
- **Notifications (optional):** if you turn on notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can tell you about new family activity. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

**Information collected automatically**
- **Sign-in and security data:** a session token kept in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain), plus technical logs that our hosting providers keep (such as IP address, request time, and device/browser type). These are used for security, abuse prevention, and keeping the Service running.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and we do not collect your contacts, health, or financial information.

## 3. How we use information
- To provide the Service: sign you in, show your circles' journals, store and deliver your media, send invitations and sign-in codes, and send notifications you turn on.
- To keep the Service safe: prevent abuse, review reports, enforce our Terms, and secure accounts.
- To support you and to tell you about important changes to the Service or this policy.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not share your content with third-party AI services.

## 4. Who can see your content
- **Your circles:** a post shared with a circle is visible to that circle's current members. "Just me" posts are visible only to you.
- **Organizers** of a circle can manage its members (for example invite or remove someone).
- **Us:** a small number of authorized people may access content only when needed to operate or secure the Service, to respond to a report or your request, or when the law requires it.
